Our history in dates

From the Talas city dairy of 1964 to the cooperative's Combine today, with the bottles, cartons and bags of each period. Many of them are kept in the Combine's hall and shown in Products and Production, 1964–2022.

  1. 14 September 1964

    The Talas city dairy opens beside the railway in the western industrial district, under the Ministry of the Meat and Dairy Industry of the Iradi SSR. Milk is bottled in half-litre glass bottles with foil caps: silver for milk, green for kefir. Bottles are returned to the shop for a deposit.

  2. 1968

    Milk goes into the paper pyramid cartons of the time; the bottles stay for kefir and smetana. The first starter cultures still used in our fermented products are kept from these years.

  3. 12 June 1972

    The kymyz shop opens to supply the sanatoriums of the Iradi SSR with mare's milk from the state farms of the Talas Alatau. Kymyz travels in churns by rail and is never sold in the city shops.

  4. 1979

    Milk in film bags reaches Talas. The pyramid carton stays for school milk.

  5. 10 April 1992

    The siege of Talas begins. The dairy keeps running on its own boilers; what milk reaches the city goes to the hospitals and children's homes. From the summer, milk is sold from the tanker in the courtyards: bring your own can.

  6. 15 April 1997

    The dairy is privatized. Shares are offered first to the farms that supply it, and more than nine hundred families pool their savings as the Talas Valley Milk Cooperative (Talas Öröönü Süt Kooperativi).

  7. 2 July 2001

    The cooperative owns the Combine outright. The first refrigerated collection points open in four villages. The blue bag with the mountain line and the words Talas Süt replaces the plain printed film of the Soviet years.

  8. 18 May 2009

    Pasteurized kymyz goes into glass bottles under the Ak Bee name and into city supermarkets for the first time.

  9. 9 October 2014

    The new fermentation hall for airan, qatiq and suzma opens; beet suzma goes into 400 g tubs.

  10. 16 April 2018

    Damira Sharipova, in the laboratory since 1996, is appointed General Director by the Cooperative Board.

  11. 3 June 2019

    The rebuilt laboratory tests every tanker before it is unloaded. The school milk programme, cut back that spring, ends.

  12. 8 November 2022

    Products and Production, 1964–2022, the history of the Combine written with retired staff, is published.

  13. 14 September 2024

    Sixty years in Talas: a liturgy of thanksgiving in the processing hall and a milk-and-boorsok table for staff and member families.

  14. 18 August 2026

    Beet suzma reaches the shops of Nurzhan.

  15. 12 October 2026

    School milk returns to 140 Talas schools: a 200 ml carton of milk or kefir for every pupil in years one to four.
